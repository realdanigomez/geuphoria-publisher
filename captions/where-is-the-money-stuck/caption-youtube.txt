Three things can break your coaching business. Here's how to find which one is yours.

Most coaches try to fix all three at once. You only need to find where the money is stuck.

No leads coming in? That's a leads problem.

Leads coming in, but they don't buy? That's an offer problem.

Clients who buy, then churn fast and leave bad reviews? That's a delivery problem.

Think about it like a client who comes to you and says my whole body hurts.

You don't tell them to quit. You find out exactly what's causing it, and you fix that one thing.

Do the same with your business.
