Most online fitness coaches could not recite these 4 numbers about their own business if you asked them right now. The ones at the top of the niche can.

Number one. Client retention rate. The percentage of clients still paying you 6 months after they started. What good looks like is 60 percent or higher at 6 months. Below that, your onboarding has a leak.

Number two. Average client lifetime value. Monthly rate times average months retained. What good looks like is 8 to 12 times your monthly price. Below 8 times, you are losing them too fast to ever get profitable on paid ads.

Number three. Hours per client, per week. What good looks like is 2 to 3 hours per client, per week, max. Anything more is not a capacity problem. It is a systems gap.

Number four. Referral rate. The percentage of new clients who came in through someone you already coached. What good looks like is 30 percent or higher. Anything less means your clients are not getting results worth talking about.

These 4 numbers do not just measure your business. They diagnose it.

Calculate them this Sunday. Whichever one is lowest is what to fix this month.
