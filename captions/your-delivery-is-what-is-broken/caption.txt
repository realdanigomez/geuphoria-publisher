Having clients isn't the problem. Delivering to them by hand is.

If you feel like you're the salesperson, the editor, the trainer and the admin all at the same time, your delivery is what's broken.

So write down every task you do by hand each week, and circle the three that eat most of your time.

For most coaches that's writing plans, doing check ins, and onboarding clients.

Then for each one: make a template, build a simple SOP, and automate and delegate as much as you can.

Fix that and you can take on more clients, get better results, more testimonials and better reviews, and it feeds right back into more clients. All without burning out.
