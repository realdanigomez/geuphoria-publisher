You have clients and no time. Here's what's actually broken.

It's your delivery.

That's when you have clients, but the work is burning you out. Check ins all day. A client texting you at 9pm on a Sunday. Every workout and nutrition plan written from scratch.

If you feel like you're the salesperson, the editor, the trainer and the admin all at the same time, your delivery system is broken.

Here's how to fix it.

If you're doing one on one coaching, either hire a team or move to one to many.

Then write down every task you do by hand every week, and circle the three that take up most of your time. For most coaches that's writing plans, doing check ins, or onboarding clients.

For each one, make a template, build a simple SOP, then automate and delegate as much as you can.

Once delivery is fixed you can take on more clients, get better results, more testimonials and better reviews, and that feeds right back into more clients. All without burning out.
