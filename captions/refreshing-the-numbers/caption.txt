You can check your Stripe ten times a day and feel like you're running your business.

But you're not. That's just anxiety pretending to be work.

Because a number you can't change right now won't move because you stared at it.

So check your numbers once a week, on purpose, to make a decision. Then put that attention back on the work, post the content, run the calls, coach your clients, reach out to new leads.

That's how the anxiety stops running your business, and you start running it instead.
