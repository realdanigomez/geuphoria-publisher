Every coach who wants to quit thinks they picked the wrong business.

You've got it backwards.

You don't have a quitting problem, you didn't pick the wrong business,

You just have a bottleneck you need to solve.

And it could be one of your foundations your leads, your clients, or your offer.

Find the one thing that's broken and don't stop until you fix it.

And if you still want to quit after that, then the decision is yours.
