An uncoachable client treats coaching like a customer service job.

8PM DMs expecting a reply. Arguing about your programming. Telling you what to do — like they're the coach. Wanting to be your best transformation in just one week.

Coachable clients respect the process, read your messages, do the homework, show up, and trust your work.

Coachable clients make you and your business better. Uncoachable clients do the opposite.

This is filter #3 of the 3-filter framework that separates the clients who pay you back from the clients who burn you out:

#1 — Pain with urgency
#2 — Payability
#3 — Coachability
