At $20K a month the temptation to automate everything in your coaching business is high. But there's one problem when you do this.

I've observed online fitness coaches at this point. The ones who keep their clients past 18 months don't automate the hard part.

Hard moments like → a client injury setback. A client getting emotional during a check-in session. A client slacking off or going through a hard time.

Those aren't admin jobs. They are leadership roles.

Automate the admin around them. But show up live for the moment.

The clients who stay more than 18 months with you, stay because of how you showed up for them at their hardest moment.

AI still isn't advanced to the point of understanding hardship and emotions on a deeper level like this. That's why you have to show up for leadership roles like these. Becuae if you don't, neither will your clients.
