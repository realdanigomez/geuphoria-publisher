Your first 10 clients as an online fitness coach. Starting from 0 with no following.

Clients 1-3: your existing network. Friends. Gym partners. Old clients. Coworkers. Start where the trust already exists.

Clients 4-6: referrals from your first 1-3 clients. This only happens if you actually delivered on your promise. Don't ask after they leave, ask when they see the progress.

Clients 7-8: cold outreach + more referrals. Test outreaches, iterate, use what works, repeat. You will fail a lot. Fail, improve. Fail again, improve. Repeat forever and you will beat everyone.

Client 9: a podcast appearance or guest post. Borrow someone else's audience.

Client 10: comes from content. So will all the rest, if you know what you're doing and you let compounding work.

Get your first 10 clients. The client-getting game gets easier after that.
