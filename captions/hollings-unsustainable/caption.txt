Most online fitness coaches who scale had 18 months of unsustainable work nobody warned them about. And most quit at year 3.

I've studied more than 100 online fitness coaches at this point. The ones who scaled all did some version of this.

A coach told me she worked her 9-5. Came home. Went live on Instagram 3 to 4 hours every night. Posted 5 reels a week. For 18 months straight.

If you're a year in and you're not doing some version of that, you're probrably not getting the clients you want.

Here's the trap. Most online fitness coaches who run that shape for 3 years quit. The ones who survive put a deadline on it.

12 months. That's the rule. After that the unsustainable thing has to become a system →

→ Hire a content editor to chop your lives into 5 reels a week.
→ Bring in a Virtual Assistant to handle every DM that hits your inbox.
→ Switch from 4 lives a night to 2 batched posts a week.

Or you fire the unsustainable thing entirely.

Build momentum. But put a deadline on the part that eats you. Otherwise the momentum eats you.
