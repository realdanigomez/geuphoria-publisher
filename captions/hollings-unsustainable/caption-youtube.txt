Most online fitness coaches who scale had eighteen months of unsustainable work nobody warned them about. And most quit at year three.

After studying more than one hundred online fitness coaches at this point, the ones who scaled all did some version of this.

A coach told me she worked her nine-to-five. Came home. Went live on Instagram for three to four hours every single night. Posted five reels a week. For eighteen months straight. It built momentum. It was not sustainable.

The honesty most coaches will not say out loud is this. If you are a year in and you are not doing some version of that, you are probably not getting the clients you want.

Here is the trap. Most online fitness coaches who run that shape for three years quit. The ones who survive put a deadline on it.

Twelve months. That is the rule. After that the unsustainable thing has to become a system. Hire a content editor to chop your lives into five reels a week. Bring in a Virtual Assistant to handle every DM that hits your inbox. Switch from four lives a night to two batched posts a week.

Or you fire the unsustainable thing entirely.

Build momentum. But put a deadline on the part that eats you. Otherwise the momentum eats you.
