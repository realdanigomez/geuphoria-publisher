You can save 100 business videos and still be in the exact same spot 3 months later.

Because watching isn't learning...

Learning is, same situation, different behavior.

That means if you posted a YouTube video and it got 2 views and watched a video about hooks to get more views,

If the next time you post a video and you don't change your behavior based on the information you got from the video you didn't learn,

But if you did change your behavior based on that you learned.

So before you watch another business video, ask yourself "what am I going to change and apply right after this?" Then go do that thing.

(Disclaimer: This is based on what I've observed; results are not guaranteed!)
