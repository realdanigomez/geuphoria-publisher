You can redo your logo all day and your business won't move an inch.

Because that's passive work, not active work.

If you keep doing the passive work, the business stays exactly where it is. But if you do the active work first, it grows.

So before you go fix something that feels productive, ask yourself "is this getting me a client, or am I just hiding?" Then go do the scary thing first.
