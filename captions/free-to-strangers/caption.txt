"I can't give that away for free."

I used to think the same way about my own content. Hold the good stuff back, save it for the people who pay.

But it's the other way around...

Give so much free value to your audience, that they start asking to buy stuff from you.

Once they start asking, you sell them the product that will solve their problem.

And the thing is, if you don't give that value away, ChatGPT or some other coach will, so might as well do it.
