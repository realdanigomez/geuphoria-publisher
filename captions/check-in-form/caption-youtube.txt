Most online fitness coaches get ghosted by their clients because of their check-in form.

The mistake: a 20-question form nobody reads. Your client skips half the questions or doesn't fill it out at all because the effort is too high.

Here's a 5-section form to stop this from happening.

1. Physical data. 3 data points. Weight. Sleep. Energy on a scale of 1-10. Anything more, they won't fill it out.

2. Adherence. One binary question. Did you hit training and nutrition this week? Yes or no. You don't need a 73% adherence score from some app, you need the honest answer.

3. The win. One open-ended question on the one thing that went right. This is where retention lives. Clients who see their wins in writing stay paying you.

4. The blocker. One open-ended question on the one thing that got in the way. This is where the actual coaching happens.

5. The ask. One open-ended question on what they need from you this week to keep making progress. Brings self-awareness and accountability.

A good check-in form is short. It helps the coach and brings accountability to the client.
