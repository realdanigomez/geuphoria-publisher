You're losing sales calls, and it's not because of your price.

You say your price, the lead goes quiet, and that silence feels so awkward you drop the price before they even answer.

But the real reason is you've never said your price out loud to someone you're trying to sell. Not once.

So the first time you hear it live, you panic.

Like Alex Hormozi says, whoever talks first loses. So never just drop your price.

That means you turn your price into one line that lays out your whole offer, you practice it till it feels boring, then on the call you say it and you wait, until the lead speaks first.

Almost every time, they talk first. And that's how you keep your full price and a client who respects you.
