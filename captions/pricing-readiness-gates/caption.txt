You don't undercharge because of your price, you undercharge because you don't believe in it.

So a lead hesitates for one second, and you fold.

Because you can't hold a price you don't believe in yourself.

The way to fix that is to make the value real before you raise the number, and Alex Hormozi's Value Equation is the cleanest way to see it... people don't pay for your time, they pay for the outcome they want and how sure they are you can get them there.

So name the outcome, not "6 months of coaching" but "down 40lbs by summer, finally confident shirtless."

Then show one real proof it works, a client result or your own.

Then ask yourself the honest one, would you pay that price, with your own money, for that outcome?

If it's a yes, the value is there, so charge it.

If it's a no, the value isn't there so deliver the value for the price you charge.
