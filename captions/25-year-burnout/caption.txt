As a coach at $20K/mo your bottleneck isn't going to be capacity anymore. It becomes how many other people's problems you can absorb.

A coach with 25 years of experience told me he's burned out. Not by the work but by the people.

The work wasn't the problem for him. It was the late-night DMs about cheat meals. The voice notes about their boyfriend or girlfriend. The check-ins where clients cry about their body, their job, their kids.

Carrying around 200 lives for 25 years sounds good but it's hard, stressfull and burns most people out.

The coaches I studied who lasted more than 10 years all did the following 2 things →

1. They wrote a list of what they don't carry. Cheat meal guilt. Their fight with their boyfriend. Their bad day at work. They tell every new client up front: "I do fitneess and nutrition coaching. The rest stays out of our DMs."

2. Or they built an exit. Hired a second coach, a Virtual Assistant, built a team that handles everyting they don't like in the business while they keep doing what they love which most of the time is coaching.

This is what the $20K/mo online fitness coaches need to scale past this point.
