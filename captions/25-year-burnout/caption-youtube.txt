As a coach at twenty thousand a month, your bottleneck is not going to be capacity anymore. It becomes how many other people's problems you can absorb.

A coach with twenty-five years of experience told me he is burned out. Not by the work but by the people.

The work was not the problem for him. It was the late-night DMs about cheat meals. The voice notes about their boyfriend or girlfriend. The check-ins where clients cry about their body, their job, their kids.

Carrying around two hundred lives for twenty-five years sounds good, but it is hard, stressful, and burns most people out. That is the unspoken cost of staying the only inbox at the top of this business.

The coaches I studied who lasted more than ten years all did the following two things.

The first move is the list of what you do not carry. Cheat meal guilt. Their fight with their boyfriend. Their bad day at work. You write it down, and you tell every new client up front: I do fitness and nutrition coaching. The rest stays out of our DMs. The clients who need a therapist find a therapist. The clients who need a coach get the best version of you.

The second move is the exit. Not exiting coaching. The structural kind. They hired a second coach. They brought in a Virtual Assistant. They built a small team that handles everything in the business they do not like, while they keep doing what they love, which most of the time is the coaching itself.

This is what the twenty thousand a month online fitness coaches need to scale past this point. The ones who do not build one of these two moves do not stay at the top for long.
