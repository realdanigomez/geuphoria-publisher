When I ask online fitness coaches what their biggest problem is, they almost always give me one of four answers.

One, they think they do not post enough. Two, they think their niche is wrong. Three, they think their offer is priced too high. Four, they think they need better leads.

Every single coach I studied tried to fix the thing they believed was wrong. Not one of those fixes worked for a single coach.

If they had been right about the problem, the solution would have produced results. It did not. Which means the problem they identified was not the real problem.

The actual problem is the order they are doing things in. Niche first, then offer, then content, then ads. Most coaches start at content and wonder why nothing else lines up.
