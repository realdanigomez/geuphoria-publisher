An offer is not a list of services. It is not online coaching with weekly check-ins. An offer is a specific outcome with a clear before and after.

An example of a bad offer is "I help busy moms get healthy."

A good offer looks more like this. "I help moms with kids under five lose fifteen pounds in twelve weeks without giving up dinners with their family. They go from feeling exhausted by three in the afternoon to having energy through bedtime. Guaranteed or you do not pay."

The difference is night and day. The second one tells you exactly what changes, in what time, and what stays protected. People pay for outcomes they can picture.

If you cannot say your offer in one paragraph with a clear before and after, you do not have an offer yet.
