Most online fitness coaches lose their new client somewhere between day 14 and day 60. The cause almost always traces back to a thin onboarding window in the first 14 days.

Here are the 5 touch-points to send a new client in their first 14 days, and when.

Day 1. Welcome message and intake form, sent within one hour of payment. The form has 5 questions, not 20.

Day 3. First program drop, paired with a 60-second voice memo. The program is the deliverable. The voice memo is the trust. A PDF cannot replace 60 seconds of you, by name, telling the client what week 1 is going to feel like and why.

Day 7. First written check-in. Same format every week. One win. One struggle. One question. One number. The client replies within 24 hours, or you follow up. No exceptions in week 1.

Day 10. A pulse-check. 60 seconds. No agenda. Just how is it actually feeling so far. That message is the difference between a client who stays and a client who quietly drifts.

Day 14. First 30-minute video review call. Walk through the data. Adjust the program. Then ask: is there one win from these 14 days you would be okay with me sharing publicly. That is the referral seed, planted before the client even hits the result.

Build these 5 touch-points as templates tonight. Use them on the next client. Retention stops being a mystery once the first 14 days are tight.
