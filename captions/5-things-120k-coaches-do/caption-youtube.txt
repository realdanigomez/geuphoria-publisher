A coach makes $120K a year working 50+ hour weeks.

From what I've seen across 100+ coaches, here are 5 patterns that separate the $120K coaches from the $10K ones.

1. They track hours against revenue. Most $10K coaches can't name their hourly rate. If you can't, the only way to make more money is to add more hours, because your income is chained to your calendar.

2. They land in a middle client range. $10K coaches usually have too few clients to pay their bills, or too many to coach them properly. That's how you end up at 50+ hour weeks and still feel broke. The coaches pulling $120K protect a middle client count on purpose.

3. They use group or async delivery. $10K coaches do 1-on-1 with every single client. $120K coaches run group programs and async check-ins, where 10 clients can buy the same hour of their time.

4. They raise prices on a schedule. Most $10K coaches I've studied haven't raised their price in over a year. Raise every 6 months, or inflation and your best clients will quietly drain your income while your hours stay the same.

5. They spend less time on content, not more. $10K coaches post all day and still can't fill their programs. $120K coaches post less, but every post is built to move someone toward booking a call.

Your income ceiling is about these 5 habits. Fix them to scale past this plateau.
