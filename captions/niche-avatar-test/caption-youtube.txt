An avatar is a person you can name.

Not busy moms. A specific busy mom. First name, age, kids of specific ages, the exact stress that wakes her up at three in the morning.

If you cannot picture one real person who fits your niche, you do not have a niche. You have a hashtag.

A certification is not a niche either. Most coaches treat credentials as their differentiator instead of a specific person they serve. That is why focusing on certs does not work.

Do not move past step one until you can find a specific group of people with similarities and build an avatar inside that niche.
