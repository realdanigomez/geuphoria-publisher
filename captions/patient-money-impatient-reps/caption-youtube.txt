Most coaches quit right before their business starts taking off,

All because they're patient with the wrong thing.

They are patient with the inputs and impatient with the outputs when it should be the other way around (let me explain...)

Your money is the output. It comes slow and you can't control it.

Your reps are the inputs, the posts, the DMs, the calls. Those are instant and you control them.

So be patient with your output (money), and impatient with your inputs (reps).
