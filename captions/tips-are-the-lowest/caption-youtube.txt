Most online fitness coaches post tips. And tips are the lowest form of content.

Here is why. Tips do not prove a niche. Tips do not prove an offer. Tips prove that you read an article. That is it.

If your content has nothing to prove, it is just posting for the sake of posting. And posting more noise on top of bad content does not fix the problem underneath.

The fix is simple. Post stories about the specific person from step one. Post proof from the offer in step two. Every single piece of content you make should either prove that you understand the person or prove that the outcome is real and repeatable.

That is the difference between content that converts and content that adds to the noise.
