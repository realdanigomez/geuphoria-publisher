When your content isn't working, don't fix everything. Fix one thing.

My views were low, so I went looking for the one number that would tell me why.

It was my skip rate. It was high, which means people were showing up and leaving in the first few seconds, so the problem was my hook.

So that's all I'm working on right now. I studied a bunch of hook videos, put it all in one Google Doc, and I'm following that for a full week of content.

Fix the hook, the skip rate drops, the views go up. Then I look for the next bottleneck.

You only need one number and one thing to fix at a time.
