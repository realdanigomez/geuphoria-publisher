Ads is the last step for one reason. Ads kill what you already have, what already works. They do not fix what does not.

If your content already converts organically, meaning you post and people DM you and a portion of those become clients, then ads pour fuel on a fire that is already burning.

If your content does not convert organically, ads will not save you. Ads will bleed money faster and scale a bad niche, offer, and content, which will hurt your reputation.

From what I have seen in my research, most online fitness coaches spend about two thousand dollars a month on ads with zero clients to show for it. Not because ads were bad. Because they never built a niche, an offer, and content underneath them.

The order is niche, then offer, then content, and then ads.
