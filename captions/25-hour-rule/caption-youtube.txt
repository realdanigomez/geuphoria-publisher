Most online fitness coaches working fifty hours a week charge their clients about half of what they should. Not because the work is not real, but because nobody has shown them the math that proves it.

After studying more than one hundred online fitness coaches at the scaling stage, the coaches who break out of the trade-hours-for-dollars trap all run the same simple test before they raise prices.

Take your monthly revenue target. Divide it by one hundred hours of work in a month. That gives you your honest hourly, the rate you are actually charging once you account for every call, every program write, every check-in, and every late reply.

Now divide that same target by twenty-five hours. That second number is the rate that gives you your life back. The coaches who hit twenty thousand a month while still seeing their family priced for the second number, not the first.

A quick example. A fifteen thousand dollar a month target divided by one hundred hours equals one hundred fifty dollars an hour. The same target divided by twenty-five hours equals six hundred dollars an hour. The gap between those two numbers is your price problem.

And yes, you can charge that. The coaches doing fifteen thousand at twenty-five hours are not smarter. They are not better trainers. They priced for the life they wanted to live, not the hours they happened to have.

Run the math tonight. If the gap between your honest hourly and your life-back rate is more than twenty percent, raise your prices in the next thirty days. Pick the date now, before you close this video.
