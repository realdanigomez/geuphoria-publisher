A client quit, and now you think you're a bad coach.

You're not. They just stopped feeling like they were getting anywhere.

Because the effort goes in on day one, but the body takes months to change. James Clear calls that gap the valley of disappointment.

That means your client did the work, looked in the mirror, saw the same person, and quit right before it was about to work.

So the fix isn't being a better coach, it's making them feel progress before the scale moves.

Two things do that. Text them the day they skip, so they know someone's watching. And hand them one small win every week, one logged workout, one good meal.

So before you blame yourself the next time someone leaves, ask yourself "did they feel like they were getting anywhere?" Then go give them that feeling.
