The 18 months nobody talks about in online fitness coaching.

If you've been working an 8 to 5 and then coming home to grind on your coaching business until midnight, you are not broken and you are not behind. You are in the unsustainable phase. Almost every coach who builds a real business goes through some version of it. Almost none of them post about it.

A coach told me she ran that shape for 18 months. 8 to 5. Then live 3 to 4 hours every night. The unsustainable phase is real. But it has to end.

Most coaches who run this shape for 3 years quit. The ones who survive don't survive because they're tougher. They survive because they put a deadline on it.

The deadline that works is about 12 months. After that the unsustainable thing has to become a system in your business. Or change to sustainable for a while.

After your 12 months are up, ask yourself three questions. Is the revenue from this big enough to keep doing it? Is there a system I can build to do it without me being live every night? If neither, am I willing to decrease this workload for the cost for a couple of months?
